Who we are

Considered travel.
Personal service.

At STRUT2, we understand that time is a valuable asset. We provide private transportation designed around your schedule, with professional chauffeurs and a commitment to a comfortable, attentive experience.

We serve Massachusetts, including Greater Boston and Cape Cod, with journeys throughout New England and beyond by arrangement. From airport transfers and corporate travel to college tours, private aviation transfers, and special occasions, we help you plan the transportation that fits your day.

Whether you need an hourly service, a journey between appointments, or transportation for a celebration, our team is here to discuss the details.

Our mission

Luxury, efficiency,
and attention to detail.

Our mission is to provide a transportation experience that brings together comfort, efficiency, and thoughtful service. We believe how you travel should feel as considered as where you are going.

What guides us

Three values. One approach to service.

Excellence in service

Personal attention begins with understanding your plans. We aim to make each stage of your experience clear and comfortable, from the first conversation to your final destination.

Reliability and safety

Your safety, schedule, and satisfaction guide our approach. Professional chauffeurs, care for our vehicles, and clear travel arrangements are central to our service.

Innovation and luxury

We look for ways to make private travel more comfortable and convenient, combining thoughtful amenities with an attentive approach to business travel, airport journeys, and special occasions.
