The details that matter

A considered approach to business travel.

For executives

Share your pickup time, stops, and required arrival times. Keep sensitive meeting information out of your request; we only need the details required to plan transportation.

For assistants and coordinators

Include the lead passenger, coordinator contact, number of travelers, and preferred follow-up method. Identify who should receive itinerary updates.

For changing schedules

Tell us about anticipated waiting time or multiple stops. Availability, vehicle choice, and pricing are confirmed for your itinerary before booking.

Before you book.

Please book at least 24 hours before pickup. For shorter notice, contact STRUT2 to confirm availability and the applicable last-minute booking fee.

A quote request is not a confirmed reservation. Your service and price remain subject to STRUT2 confirmation.
