Website privacy

Your quote details.

The homepage form prepares a request in your browser. It does not automatically submit your information to STRUT2. Choosing the email action passes the prepared request to your email application; you must send the email yourself. Copying the request places it on your device clipboard.

Only share information needed to arrange your transportation. Do not include payment card details, identification documents, or confidential business information in the form or email.

Visitor preferences.

This website stores a small first-visit flag in your browser to avoid repeatedly showing the welcome offer. It does not contain your name or trip details. Clearing browser storage or using a different browser may show the offer again.

Website services.

This version does not include Google Analytics or visitor-notification tracking. Website hosting and external resources, including images and fonts, may receive technical request information such as your IP address. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies.

External booking and flight status.

Reservation buttons open Limo Anywhere's booking website. Flight-status links open FlightAware. Those services operate separately and have their own privacy terms. Checking a flight does not update a reservation.

Questions.

For questions about information you share with STRUT2, email marketing@strut2.com. Confirm STRUT2's customer-data retention and handling arrangements before sharing sensitive information.
